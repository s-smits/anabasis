# Transfer ledger: v1 to v2

This ledger tracks each part of v1's loop: what it did, and whether v2 has it. v1 is harness-builder-v4 at `7af7315`; read its files with `git show 7af7315:<path>`. Paths below sit under v1's `src/` unless they start with `tools/`, `vendor/`, `starters/` or `packages/`. `plan:<line>` cites the plan of record, "Anabasis one plan".

Each session walks v1's loop and builds the gap that most delays a live end-to-end round. It proves that gap with faux tests, then with a cheap live run, and lands it as a stacked PR. Then the session updates the row here.

Statuses:
- **ported**: copied or sed from v1, cited at the copy.
- **replaced**: a v2 mechanism does the job differently.
- **dropped**: cut on purpose, with the plan line that cuts it.
- **missing**: needed and not built.
- **unsure**: the plan doesn't settle it.
- **deferred**: behind a plan gate, and not built before it. Promotion and the controller loop wait on gate G5, a step against the reference harness, which is the Starter Built Harness until a step is promoted (`docs/plan.md`, Phases 5 and 6).

Two facts frame the rest:
- **v1 has no "rollback".** The word never occurs. A rollback is a promotion decision of "held", recorded as `recordProductDecision({adopt:false})`, which keeps the product selected before.
- **v1 has no head-to-head comparison.** v1 measures each candidate on its own Builder-written battery and promotes it by clauses: unmeasured, claim-refused, zero-verified, unbound task set or evaluator, stale identity, fingerprint drift.

A row marked **open #N** is built in pull request #N. #2 to #15 are on main since 8 October (`b26e531`), so a row that names one of them is on main; #17 and later are the open stack above it.

## 2. Builder session

The Builder is `src/builder/session.ts`, `tools.ts` and `prompt.ts` (open #5, #13). `build()` runs one session from a parent harness state, which is a Built Harness. It copies the parent whole into the Builder's home as `harness/`. When the session ends, it freezes what `harness/` holds, a child Built Harness, into `<states>/<digest>`. `round()` (open #8) then screens and scores the child against the parent.

A second Builder on the local branch `claude/builder-session` did the same job and does not land. Its live run, a kept child of c01 and two solves under it, was the first live evidence of a Builder.

Two Builder sessions on the Starter Built Harness (9 October, Opus at high) both ended `finished` with a changed harness. On buffer: 16 minutes, 37 turns, 4 practice solves, $1.73. It built PHREEQC from source into the harness's tool tree and added one tool that evaluates a candidate; under the new harness 18 of 18 solves called that tool and none fetched an engine, where 18 of 18 under the starter fetched one themselves. On truss: 103 minutes, 46 turns, all 6 practice solves, $3.38, with a checker of the public rules as a program in the tool tree and no new tool. Only the buffer Builder defined a tool, and three of its checks failed on the tool file's shape before one opened: the session holds no account of that interface beyond the starter's one tool.

### The Starter rewrite of 9 October

Until then a v2 solver was v1's Built shell: `bash` with v1's description, v1's six starter tools and an artifact-writer, under Claude Code's prompt with a paragraph and a tool roster appended. It is now pi's coding agent as pi releases it, with v2's choices at four seams: the command wall behind every tool's operations, the answer as a file, the duty and the wall's facts in pi's own prompt slots, and the harness's guide as pi's project context. What decided each change was read from the solves on record: 209 finished solves in six campaigns of 8 and 9 October (buffer, truss, recode and the pr55 pilot), 62 of them under the Starter at a two-hour wall.

| What the logs showed | Count | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| A command began by leaving its fresh folder for the home (`cd ~`) | 384 of 422, 477 of 558, 223 of 231 and 446 of 495 bash calls in the four campaigns' two-hour Starter solves | One working directory, the home, for every tool |
| Files were written through the shell | heredoc-writing commands held most of a solver's command text: a median of 33 to 61 thousand characters a truss solve | pi's `write`, `edit` and `read` |
| `restore_candidate` and `inspect_draft` were never called | 0 calls in 209 solves; `save_candidate` 39, `preview_artifact` 53 | The four draft tools dropped |
| `read_public_resources` was one call that opened a solve | 189 calls; the first call in all 62 two-hour Starter solves | The rules are files; the prompt says where |
| An answer was computed into a file, printed, and typed into the writer | on truss 38 of 41 writer calls followed a command that printed the answer, of 3 to 8 KB | The answer is the file |
| A full volume locked its solver out | one solve at 1.33 GB of 2 GiB: `rm -rf ~/w` refused 14 times, each `ENOSPC` before the command started | A command with nothing staged starts in the home |
| A Builder had no account of the tool interface | the first buffer session wrote three tool files to print `defineDraftTool`'s source through tool names | `HARNESS.md` in the Builder's home |

Three more things the same logs showed are not changed by the rewrite, and bear on the budget:

- The wall sized nothing. At two hours no Starter solve was ended by it, and a solve submitted at a median of 4% (buffer), 9% (recode), 19% (truss) and 30% (pilot) of it. At five minutes the median solve submitted after 93 s, and 27 of 46 were rejected.

Two holes in the budget's reading were closed with the rewrite. The rewrite is a new condition. The Starter has another digest and every solver reads another prompt, so no earlier solve counts for it, and it needs its own Starter solves and its own reading of one harness against itself before anything is compared (`docs/plan.md`).

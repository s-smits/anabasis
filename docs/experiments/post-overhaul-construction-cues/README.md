## Three tiers of suspected harm

These are subjective working confidence bins, not calibrated effect probabilities. High confidence in a conditional inference error is not a 95% probability that deleting an optional component improves the complete system. The final overhaul may already repair a historical issue.

### Roughly 95%+: conditional integrity or inference harms

1. **Evaluator defects, unpublished rules or wall stops counted as intrinsic demand.** Owners: evaluator/verifier, review settlement and `src/run/climb-history.ts`. The supplied S1/S6 account distinguishes these outcomes, and #111 adds accepted-wall reporting. Preserve raw outcomes and case-specific evidence; do not ablate correctness controls or loosen settlement merely to obtain a partial battery.
2. **A task-specific expected-response service or private grading material available to the solver.** Owners: generated agent tools and the publication contract. The historical `expected_behaviour` copy motivates #111's two-route boundary. Keep it. Native compilers/simulators, public primitives and solver-written tests are not automatically graders. A contract violation and private-information leakage are separate findings.
3. **Proxy-to-difficulty assertions.** `examples.md` calls a few-minute pass a small job regardless of the task and treats agreement with a stored answer as evidence of one obvious answer. Efficient tooling or a difficult unique optimum are counterexamples. `harness-trial.ts` also forecasts a battery from one chosen trial. The implication is unjustified; its downstream behavioural effect is not measured. B tests the smallest isolated portion.
4. **Mandatory search budgets and moving-condition comparisons.** The universal witness budget is already removed. Do not revive it as the default or compare runs by current branch names. These are experiment-integrity conditions, not invitations to disable safeguards.

Baseline regression to check separately: #111's new wall counter adds near-wall unaccepted rows to all `pass:false` rows with `SOLVE_WALL_MESSAGE`. A row satisfying both predicates would count twice. Predicate overlap is source-visible; reachability through the actual reader has not been executed here. Test before freezing and fix any confirmed defect uniformly in the baseline, never in one arm alone.

### Roughly 80%: strong suspects worth controlled removal

5. **Universal initial competing-margin method.** `INTENT_CLAUSE` requires several requirements to spend one another's margin. This can steer conformance work toward invented resource constraints instead of authentic behaviour. The historical observations do not establish it as a universal route to difficulty. Selected as A; coupled work remains available.
6. **Single-trial score forecasts.** `trialNextAction` and the trial description turn a selected task's outcome into a near-zero/near-full forecast. A Builder-selected task is not a random sample of its family. Selected as B, preserving the useful actual measurement.
7. **Changing every passed task.** `noLimitLine` says to carry none forward unchanged. This may force churn and discard useful anchors. Avoiding repeated easy-task spend is a legitimate counterargument. A later arm should remove only the universal instruction, preserving task counts, freshness rules and changed-subset accounting; do not replace it with an anchor quota.

### Roughly 60%: plausible but two-way hypotheses

8. **Worked-example reminder anchoring.** Owner: `roundClause` and session `tellOnce`. Removing only the reminder may reduce anchoring or may remove useful access. Keep the file, initial STARTER link and tool availability identical; judge task quality, not reads alone.
9. **Simulator discoverability constraining scope.** The exact owner awaits the final #111 discovery surface. Explicit guidance may anchor tasks to convenient instrument coverage; alternatively it may be necessary to reach that coverage. Remove only the pointer, never the installed instrument, help, PATH or permissions. Not instantiated before the instrument is final.
10. **Grounding ceremony or selective confirmation.** Owners: STARTER, contract.md and EPOCH_REVIEW_PROMPT. Test the retention offer separately from source-review emphasis. Keep ordinary research, storage, hashing, source access and publication boundaries. Do not erase evidence to emulate lighter instructions.
11. **Optional rehearsal cost or discovery-model adaptation.** Owners include registration, RehearsalTraces, onRehearsal, review inputs and prompts. Suppressing one return string is not a full information ablation because other channels remain. Separate disclosure from availability/cost. Preserve correctness_check, controls and F2; defer this larger intervention until B and transfer evidence.
12. **In-flight review cadence.** AuthoringReviews coalesces triggers, runs asynchronously and joins at submit. High overhead is not established by source alone. Profile spend, unique actionable findings and blocking wait before a backstop-only ablation; preserve required validation and completed-battery review.
13. **Optional advisers masking toolkit contribution.** This is a separate fixed-battery diagnostic. Useful advisers may be essential to the product; fewer passes after removing them is not better construction. Keep readers/writers/submit, public obligations, compilers, simulators and scoring fixed.

## Common-baseline experiment contract

Proposed first screen: control/A/B, two independent starts per arm in each of two unlike domains, for twelve construction campaigns. This is a screening design, not a powered causal conclusion or authorization to spend. Firmware and trusses provide unlike construction problems. Use the same literal one-line request within every domain; preserve the historical multi-board firmware scope rather than silently calling it ESP32-only. Any normalized one-line truss request is a new request identity.

At freeze, fill exact Builder/Built/reviewer model and effort pins, fixed provider/cost and wall caps, initial source/context/tool seed and resource conditions. Do not guess unresolved model identities. Use a finite four-round maximum per campaign, with the first two accepted batteries the primary construction window. All starts, failures to submit and incomplete measurements remain in the report; review cannot replace an inconvenient early battery with a later one.

Create sibling worktrees from the same final baseline and immutable initial data. Use independent writable state and no inherited lessons between arms. Match memory/transcript state, models/efforts, concurrency, host/tool environment, outer resource ceilings, validation, measurement policy and stop rules. Randomize or counterbalance run order; do not put one arm systematically under more host contention. Actual Builder-selected solver settings remain generated outcomes: record them, and do not call a reduced solve wall greater task demand.

Record source commit/executable digest, served prompts/rosters, starter and context digests, candidate/bundle/task/scoring identities, installed tool trees and execution receipts, model identities/efforts, isolation, resource limits and terminations. A patch applying cleanly is insufficient if the surrounding final system changed.

Generated tasks, harnesses and checks are outcomes of construction. They need not be identical across arms, but their raw pass rates are not paired measurements of the same exam. Compare construction-policy yields, coverage and cost under matched starts and independent validity assessment. Use paired case comparisons only where identities support them.

## Scorecard and interpretation

| Outcome | Required report |
|---|---|
| Validity | Valid alternatives, meaningful wrong-artifact probes, request-to-executed-obligation trace and limits of source verification |
| Supported coverage | What each observation path actually exercises, by capability and board; not merely declared checks |
| Valid-demand failure | An accepted artifact failing a published obligation for supported task-demand reasons, not an instrumentation/publication/wall outcome |
| Boundary yield | Starts yielding a frozen battery with both a verified pass and supported valid-demand failure, divided by all starts |
| Solve effort | Medians, tails and maximum; all attempts and by outcome; tools, spend, compile/simulate/wait components and termination receipts |
| Construction cost | Authoring, reference work, rehearsals, review, refusals and failed starts, not successful sessions alone |
| Throughput | Submitted, accepted and completed batteries separately; missing/unresolved results and time to first supported boundary |
| Further climb | Subsequent sound increases in authentic demand; comparable difficulty only under matched product/scoring/resource conditions |

Keep raw outcomes, kernel-eligible outcomes and offline validity assessment side by side. Instrument defects, publication gaps, actual wall stops, provider/environment non-results, unsuccessful unaccepted attempts and unresolved cases stay distinct. Explanatory issues may coexist; do not force a causal classification. No reviewer settlement is not proof of correctness. Offline labels do not rewrite ledgers or enter Builder memory.

A compiler pass proves compilation; a real CPU core does not certify peripheral models. Authored wrappers are not inherently defective. Case-specific evidence is required for the investigated obligation. Preserve controls, F2, public obligations, private grading information, Wilson calculations and zones.

## Confirmation and held-outs

Discovery rehearsals and batteries can guide only their own campaign. They are not fresh confirmation of tasks selected using them. Choose the first qualifying candidate within the finite window by the prespecified validity/coverage rule, not the lowest score. Freeze tasks, scoring, toolkit and resources before fresh sessions of the discovery solver without revision.

Run a preselected held-out solver separately. Keep its results/artifacts outside all Builder contexts, notes and revision decisions. A human using holdout results to redesign the study turns them into development evidence; a new holdout is then needed. No qualifying candidate remains a non-qualification, not a reason to extend the search until one appears. Report disappearing failures, ceiling effects and uncertainty.

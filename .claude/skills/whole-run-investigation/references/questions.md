# Operator questions

These 55 standing questions express what the operator checks across investigations and releases, distilled from recurring operator questions across Claude and Antigravity sessions, the design philosophy, end-to-end stack contracts, and the v1.0 graduation bar. Every question is positively framed: a primary-source `yes` across all 55 defines a system that climbs honestly, transfers out-of-distribution, stays simple, and holds up in production.

`wri.ts start` copies the `## Questions` table below into each investigation's `shared-instructions.md`, under `## Operator questions`. Each lane named in a row answers that question with a `Question <id>: <yes | partial | no | unmeasured>, <count, denominator, and reader>` line under `## Questions`, and `collect` refuses a report that leaves one out or omits its status word. Where a single run or campaign log cannot settle a cross-run or external-benchmark question, the lane opens with `unmeasured` and names the owed read. `reports.md` computes the scorecard header, flags cross-lane conflicts, and gathers every lane's answer per question for the synthesis.

## Adding and changing questions

- **For every later investigation:** add or edit a row in the table below, naming one or more open lanes (`2` to `19`).
- **For one investigation only:** edit that investigation's `## Operator questions` section before `wri.ts lanes`.

A row added after a lane's prompt was built is not in that prompt, yet `collect` still asks it of that lane: rebuild the lane, or have its report answer the row.

The blind lane `1` and the isolated lane `20` answer none: neither may see the shared framing. Every other lane (`2` to `19`) answers at least one (`test/wri-readers.test.ts`).

## Pillars

| pillar | ids | what "yes" establishes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Climb and compounding | Q1 to Q7 | Multi-step capability gains that hold on unseen batches, compound across promotions, beat prior releases cleanly, and replicate in cold domains |
| 2. External transfer and systems | Q8 to Q14 | Built harnesses beat `bareSolver` and one-pass authors on sealed external benchmarks, approach supervised baselines, port across OSes, and let cheaper solvers match dearer ones |
| 3. Examiner ambition and supply | Q15 to Q21 | Tasks get genuinely harder after each promotion, preserve real headroom, stay grounded in engines or truth, span the prompt, and carry non-saturated rubric signal |
| 4. Statistical honesty and isolation | Q22 to Q29 | Pooled A/A false steps stay inside the statistical bar, wins clear rollout noise and multiple looks, gaming channels are closed in code, and the information wall holds |
| 5. Verifier, audit, and judge calibration | Q30 to Q35 | Passes and rejections match blind human labels, checks accept equivalent valid answers, the Auditor is consistent, draft audits catch real holes, and two-call swapped judging with ground truth resists bloat |
| 6. Harness quality and Builder agency | Q36 to Q41 | Shipped tools causally win tasks, guides tell the truth, the Builder diagnoses and practises honestly across compactions, and prompts match files without domain leakage |
| 7. Economy and speed to signal | Q42 to Q45 | Build cost and active hours per confirmed promotion trend down, serving cost stays within starter spread, dead waits are rare, and limits do not distort slow domains |
| 8. Resilience, simplicity, and rigour | Q46 to Q50 | Requested backends and sandboxes hold without silent fallback, runs record every ending and recover cleanly, code stays single-owner and minimal, and cycles close on pre-registered predictions |
| 9. End-to-end stack contracts | Q51 to Q55 | Cross-seam contracts from [stack-contracts.md](../../run-improvement-campaign/references/stack-contracts.md) hold across draft-try headroom, seat working directories, judged-lane family pairing, external toolchain transfer, and full `referee.turn` serving |

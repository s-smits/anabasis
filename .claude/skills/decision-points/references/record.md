## Duration, cost & stops

- **To promo 1**: median 7.8h (2.0–31.2h, n=88); ≥3 Nov: 5.4h (n=26).
- **To promo 2**: median 12.7h (4.0–52.4h, n=49); ≥3 Nov: 11.4h (n=16).
- **Promos 3–7**: median 4–7 elapsed h (2–3.4 active h), \$33–\$61 billed each.
- **At manual stop**: median 17.1h (IQR 11.3–28.1, n=67); ≥3 Nov: 12.9h (n=21). From latest promo: 3.1h (n=51); from promo 2: 6.3h (n=30; ≥3 Nov: 4.1h, n=12).
- **Zero-promo endings**: median 9.2h, 2 comparisons (n=52), max \$186 on current meter.
- **Starter confirmation reported at stop**: 16/51 overall, 14/17 (≥3 Nov), 8/8 (≥9 Nov, after `--after reported`).
- **Non-manual endings**: 15 rule-based (4 budget, 4 patience, 3 no-room, 2 exam, 2 finished), 4 `environment`, 6 login limit, 1 live. 51 resumed runs produced 58 further promotions across 25 runs.

## Policy history (136 directives, 7 Oct–15 Nov 2026)

- 7 Oct: stop at 5–6h → 12 Oct: continue for multi-promo (3 for easy) → 25 Oct: 6 promos or 12h → 3 Nov: auto-stop at 2 (except jobstat) → 6–8 Nov: notify + pause at 2 after next report → **15 Nov (current)**: two slots (pause one at 2 promos, let the other climb to 3–4+).
- Proof gates: ≥2 promos on unchanged tree (4+ & full WRI for minor, 1 for patch). All 7 regretted actions were premature stops or an unverified restart; none were runs left active too long.
- **Limits**: stop reasons live in transcripts, elapsed hours include idle waits, pre-1 Nov spend needs repricing, 13 logs from 9–10 Oct used a legacy format excluded here.

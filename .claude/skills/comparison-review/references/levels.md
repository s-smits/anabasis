# The levels

Inspect levels 0 to 12 in strict order, recording one ledger row per level with its supporting count or noting why it could not be read. Levels 0 to 6 require no outcome scores and cover every task (including the sealed half); levels 7 to 11 inspect outcomes on the open half until unsealing. **Inside** identifies the authoritative fold for an internal campaign comparison; **outside** specifies what an external benchmark study must verify directly.

- **0. Who ran**: Verify each arm's exact harness digest against the saved checkpoint store, and confirm from solve traces which model and reasoning effort answered every turn (checking for model fallbacks, truncated sessions, swapped labels, or stale directories). **Inside:** `condition.recorded`, `bun tools/runs/degradations.ts <name>`. **Outside:** each trace's model field, termination status, and `export.json`.
- **1. Coverage**: Record what fraction of the benchmark ran (tasks, categories, rollouts per task), how and when tasks were selected, whether they fit the campaign's domain, and attempts started, retried, and excluded. **Inside:** `draw <name>`, `reading <name>`, admission line. **Outside:** study manifest and full benchmark task list.
- **2. Conditions**: Verify execution timing/order (interleaved vs separated by days), Python interpreter/packages/`PATH`, executor/tools/system prompt/wrappers, budgets and budget instructions, network access, host load, and grader model/conversion pipeline. **Inside:** `conditions <name>`, `HOST LOADED`, `LOAD MOVED`. **Outside:** runtime logs and manifests.
- **3. What was carried**: Diff the staged guide and toolchain against the saved checkpoint (`rebuilt` in `export.json` for cross-platform interpreter/wheel replacements), confirm symlinks resolve and tools execute under the solver's `cwd` and `PATH`, verify `agent/tools-spec.json` presets match, and check whether the solver loaded the guide. **Inside:** harness digest guarantees byte identity. **Outside:** `studies/shared/{bundle,foreign-python,pi-solve}.ts` and the staged directory tree.
- **4. What was told**: Classify every factual claim in the guide inside the solver's workspace (limits, interfaces, scoring rules, workflow steps, tool expectations) as true, false, or inapplicable in that runtime (including export rewrites), and check whether traces cite or act on false claims.
- **5. What it was selected on**: Identify which measure carried the promotion in its home campaign (`reading`, `replay`, `carriedBy`), whether it was confirmed on fresh tasks, how the campaign's checks compare against the external grader, refusal/limit counts, and structural asymmetries (`studies/harness2/README.md`).
- **6. What the grader could see**: Reproduce the grader's exact document conversion locally without a model: inspect character truncation caps, embedded images consuming budgets, dropped figures/notebook outputs, section ordering, and file selection. Distinguish criteria missed past a truncation cap (instrument artefact) from criteria absent from the full deliverable. **Inside:** verifier inspects the complete answer; Auditor rules with probes (lanes 1 to 5).
- **7. How the score is formed**: Inspect binary vs partial-credit grading across points, rubrics, and individual criteria while keeping the pre-registered primary measure authoritative. Count single-criterion rubric misses, single-task/rubric gap concentration, leave-one-out sign stability, and the paired sign-flip test on per-task differences (`testOf`, `src/kernel/compare.ts`).
- **8. Each split, both directions**: List every task or rubric where the arms disagreed (wins and losses), classifying each miss (blindly where possible) as **wrong**, **absent**, **unseen** (truncated before the grader saw it), or **disputed**, and tabulating shared misses separately. **Inside:** per-task inspection stays within investigation lanes; reports cite only ids and counts.
- **9. Behaviour**: Compare turn counts, wall duration, and output tokens stratified by activity (substantive work, environment recovery, file reading, polishing), plus tool/program invocations, failures, and termination kinds. On external benchmarks, `compare.ts levels` counts shell commands referencing or executing exported harness programs. **Inside:** `solve.call`, solve lanes, `chain`, `tasks`.
- **10. Noise**: Quantify intra-harness rollout variance from a clean, interleaved A/A control (or mark unmeasured), separate from grader regrading variance, alongside the p-value and minimum detectable effect (`bun .claude/skills/comparison-reading/scripts/size.ts`). **Inside:** paired A/A (`noise`), `falseRate`, `incapable`.
- **11. Economy**: Report each arm's billed dollars and wall hours alongside its score on a single meter (`billOf`), keeping grader spend separate and stating the pricing rate.
- **12. What this could not read**: List everything unobserved (unrecorded reasoning traces, missing logs, unlogged runtime conditions, unrun tasks). Absence of evidence is never a clean bill of health (`AGENTS.md` section 9).
